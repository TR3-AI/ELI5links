A department = a small team

Not the full nine. A department is a lean 6-person crew (7 if it has screens and buttons), with its own window, its own branch, and its own memory. Planning stays central; only the hands-on building fans out.

⬇️
3 🧲

Starting one is one command

One line spins up a department team in its own window. Safe by default: it shows the plan and does nothing until I add the word go. It never uses the old “fill to nine” trick — these teams are meant to be lean.

⬇️
4 🔒

Closing one needs your word

Closing a department shuts its windows only, and only when you say yes. Its branch, commits, pull requests, and chat history are always kept — nothing is ever deleted. The permanent core team never closes.

⬇️
