here's what happened 😅

sorry for the scary word — nothing was lost

1 🧠

I have a small memory

I can only hold so much of our chat in my head at once. Think of a whiteboard that only fits so much writing.

⬇️
2 📝

The whiteboard filled up

Our chat got long. When the board is nearly full, I can't keep writing on it — there's no room left.

⬇️
3 📸

So I photograph the board

Before anything gets wiped, the system takes a full photo of everything — what's done, what's left, every PR number, every rule. (That photo is the “handoff note.”)

⬇️
4 🧹

Wipe clean, tape the photo up

I get a fresh whiteboard with that photo stuck to the top. That tiny gap — old board gone, new one starting — is what I clumsily called “suspended.” Bad word choice.

⬇️
5 ▶️

I carry on — nothing lost

I read the photo and pick up exactly where I was. First thing I did: checked your 3 PRs on GitHub — all still open, nothing merged, main untouched.

Quick worries, answered 👇

✅

“Did you lose my stuff?”

No. The photo holds every decision and every PR. And I don't just trust the photo — I re-checked the real thing on GitHub before saying a word.

🔖

“Isn't that the come-back note?”

Close, but different. The come-back note is when I pause because I'm waiting on you. This one is when my whiteboard just got full. Different reason — same promise: nothing lost.

Short version: my memory filled up, so I saved everything and started fresh. Nothing changed, nothing merged.
