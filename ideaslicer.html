Idea Slicer 🍰

what you get when you call it

1 🗣️

You just talk

Dump the idea in any order, any mess. Every later thought too — no format, no structure needed. Talking is the whole job.

⬇️
2 🗺️

You get a living map page

The idea sliced into parallel departments: the operating flow, who hands what to whom, what can start now, what's blocked on you. One link, always current, made for your phone.

⬇️
3 💬

Every new thought lands on the map

Each thing you add becomes a tracked thought and gets sliced in. The map re-renders itself about a minute later — you watch it grow.

⬇️
4 🛑

Contradictions stop the line

If a new thought fights a pinned rule — like “low bundlers good” vs “increasing is better” — nothing silently changes. You get both sides quoted, and you rule.

⬇️
5 ✅

Facts + FAQs on the page

Facts are locked-in truths with their sources — only what a fresh reader would get wrong without them. FAQs answer the idea in plain words. A wrong fact gets fixed at the source, everywhere.

⬇️
6 🔁

Resume anywhere

New chat, new model — one line (“resume the X idea, issue #N”) and it continues. Chat history is expendable; the repo and the issues are the memory.

⬇️
7 📖

One rulebook binds every agent

The rules live in AGENTS.md inside the repo (plus the rulebook page). Every agent — any chat, any model — follows the same rules.

Short version: you talk → it slices → the map page stays current, honest, and resumable anywhere.
