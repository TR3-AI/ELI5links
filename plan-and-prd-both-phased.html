Plan and PRD — both get phased 📚

not just the plan — and who turns a phase into real tasks

1 🙋

Your two questions

What does “the ticket-writer fills each phase” mean? And do we phase the PRD too, or just the plan?

⬇️
2 🎯

A phase is a milestone

A chunky goal — like “get login working.” Still too big to just start typing code.

⬇️
3 🎟️

The ticket-writer breaks it down

The Feature Tickets seat turns each phase into small, real tasks — tickets, each with a mini-spec and a test to pass. That's the crew's actual to-do list.

⬇️
4 📚

Both documents get phased

The Gold Plan (the roadmap) and the full PRD (the detailed needs) are cut into the same phases for every team. Not plan-only.

⬇️
5 🗣️

Now spelled out

The rule used to just say “section.” I reworded it to name both the Gold Plan slice and the PRD slice — so nobody can read it as plan-only.

⬇️
6 🛟

Done and on the shelf

Words-only, 118 tests pass, on staging for both repos. It rides the same main go-ahead already in front of you — nothing new to decide.

Short version: a phase is a milestone, the Feature Tickets seat fills it with real tasks, and both the Gold Plan and the PRD are broken into those phases — now said out loud.
