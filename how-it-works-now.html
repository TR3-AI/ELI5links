How it all works now 🗺️

you → plan → sections → build → one codebase

1 🔀

It starts with you

You talk to Ops — the one switchboard between you and every team. You → Ops → the right manager, and back the same way.

⬇️
2 📝

The plan is made once

The core team's two planners turn your idea into one full plan (the Gold Plan) and its spec (the PRD). Made once, as a whole, before anything is split.

⬇️
3 ✂️

Cut into sections

The plan is split into as many sections as it needs — no limit. Each section is a department. Only one section? No department — the core team just builds it.

⬇️
4 🚦

Three crews at a time

Only 3 teams run at once (core + 2 departments). The rest wait in a queue. When one finishes and closes, the next one starts — one out, one in. Close two, two start.

⬇️
5 🧱

Each crew builds its own piece

Every team reads the whole plan but builds only its own section, on its own branch. Nobody steps on anyone else's work.

⬇️
6 🧩

The pieces become one

Each piece rolls up — team → central staging → main — into one shared codebase. Going live on main needs your OK.

⬇️
7 🔄

Rules keep everyone in sync

Change a rule, tell Ops "push," and every running team pulls the latest. A new team starts with the current rules and tools already built in.

Short version: you → Ops → plan once → cut into sections → 3 crews build at a time → all pieces join into one; rules keep everyone current.
