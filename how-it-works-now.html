How it all works now 🗺️

idea → the Manager → plan → sections → build → one codebase

1 💡

Your idea → the Manager

A new idea goes straight from you to the core Manager, one-on-one — never through Ops. The whole planning stage is just you and the manager.

⬇️
2 🛠️

Get set up to plan

Together you pick the language, name the repo, and decide what's needed; Research prices it. You approve the cost + setup before any planning starts.

⬇️
3 📝

The plan gets written & approved

Two planners draft it (blind, then merged), through two Plannotator checkpoints with Wayfinder between. You approve the facts, then the plan at each — four approvals. Out comes the Gold Plan + PRD.

⬇️
4 ✂️

Cut into sections

The approved plan splits into as many sections as it needs — no limit. Each section is a department. Only one section? No department — the core team just builds it.

⬇️
5 🚦

Three crews at a time

Only 3 teams run at once (core + 2 departments). The rest wait in a queue. When one finishes and closes, the next starts — one out, one in. Close two, two start.

⬇️
6 🔀

Now Ops is the switchboard

While teams build, it all runs through Ops: reports and day-to-day decisions go team → Ops → you, and back. (Planning always stays direct with the manager.)

⬇️
7 🧩

It all comes together

Each piece rolls up — team → central staging → main — into one codebase (main needs your OK). Change a rule? Tell Ops "push" and every team pulls the latest.

Short version: idea → the Manager (not Ops) → plan & approve → cut into sections → 3 crews build → Ops runs the day-to-day → all pieces become one.
