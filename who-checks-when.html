Who checks it, and when? 🧑‍⚖️

once each — not the manager twice, and here's why

1 🧹

The first pass is a tidy, not a review

After the code goes green, Feature Tickets cleans its own code. Only it can — that means editing code, and the manager never touches code. So this one stays with the worker.

⬇️
2 🔍

The second pass is the real review

Judging the finished change. It asks two questions — Clean? (tidy, no smells) and Right? (does what the ticket asked). This is the one that must be independent.

⬇️
3 🤖

Clean? → the robot

Greptile scores "is it clean?" and doesn't care who wrote it — so it's already a fair, outside judge. No change needed there.

⬇️
4 🧑‍⚖️

Right? → the manager

"Did it do what the ticket asked?" goes to the manager — it knows the ticket, didn't write the code, and never codes. One independent check. Feature Tickets just keeps a quick sanity glance first.

⬇️
5 🚫

Why not the manager twice?

Two looks by the same eyes, minutes apart, is the exact redundancy you just killed on the worker. Doubling it just moves the waste to the manager. One good check beats two lukewarm ones.

🎁

A free second look, no extra step

The manager is already at the final gate with you (it approves the promotion to main). If the robot's fixes changed the code, it glances again — "still matches the ticket?" — right there. Costs nothing; that gate already exists.
