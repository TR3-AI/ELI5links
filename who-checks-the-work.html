Should a worker grade their own homework? 📝

the weak spot you found — and how to fix it

1 📝

The weak spot

Right now the same worker (Feature Tickets) writes the code, cleans it, and checks it — twice. That's marking your own homework: the eyes that made the mistake are the eyes looking for it.

⬇️
2 ✂️

It undoes our own trick

The loop splits two jobs on purpose — one worker writes the test, a different one makes it pass — so nobody clears their own bar. Then we handed the checking back to one of them. That quietly undoes the split.

⬇️
3 🤖

One honest checker already exists

The robot reviewer (Greptile) at the gate doesn't care who wrote the code, so it's a fair judge of "is it clean?" But it doesn't know what the ticket was meant to do.

⬇️
4 🎯

So here's the real gap

"Did it do what the ticket asked?" — that needs someone who knows the ticket but didn't write the code. Clean code that quietly does the wrong thing is exactly what slips through today.
