When a new team is born 👶

who says hello to Ops — and who stays quiet

1 👋

The manager says hello

The moment a team is created, its manager sends one hello to Ops. Just the manager — not each worker.

⬇️
2 📇

What the hello says

Two things: how many people are on the team, and any sub-teams (departments) — each one's name and who's in it.

⬇️
3 🗒️

Ops writes it down

He keeps the master list of who's who, so he can always tell you without guessing. A sub-team doesn't start work until Ops knows it's there.

⬇️
4 🔁

Four things the manager always tells Ops

A sub-team opens, a sub-team closes, anyone gets renamed — and he keeps his own live list current. Ops hears about each one straight away.

⬇️
5 🙊

Workers stay quiet

A worker never pings Ops “here I am”. It talks to its own manager; the manager speaks for the whole team. (The one exception: a worker may reach Ops only if its manager has gone silent over an hour.)

⬇️
6 ⚠️

What you're seeing right now

Single agents messaging Ops “here I am, this is who I am” is the old, no-rule behaviour. There is no live rule yet telling teams the clean way — that's the gap this fixes.
