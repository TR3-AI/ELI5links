Where was I?
the come-back note 🔖

how I pause a job and get back to it — nothing lost

1 🏰

I'm doing your job

Say I'm building your castle (a task). Partway through I need to ask you something — but you're busy, so no answer yet.

⬇️
2 ⏰

I wait 30 minutes

Still no answer. I don't sit around doing nothing — that would waste your time.

⬇️
3 📝

I stick a note on the castle

"Stopped here. This is what's done, this is what's left." Then I go do another job that's already OK'd, so I keep being useful.

⬇️
4 📬

Your answer comes back

That's my bell. Your answer is about the castle, so it tells me: time to go back. I stick a note on the second job too (done, or "paused here"), then head back.

⬇️
5 🏰

I carry on where I stopped

I read my castle note and pick up exactly where I left off. No guessing, nothing lost.

You asked me three things 👇

🔍

"Does anyone double-check the note?"

No. I did the work, so I'm the only one who knows where I stopped. I write it and sign it — done. It's a sticky note, not homework to be graded. (Checking whether the work itself is really finished is a different thing — we'll sort that one out together.)

🧸

"Where do the notes go?"

Each team has its own toy box (its own private memory on GitHub). Notes go in a shared drawer in that box, so any teammate can find them. Other teams have their own boxes — they don't mix.

🔔

"Do I come back on my own?"

Yes. Your answer is about the paused job, so it points me straight back — nobody has to chase me. And the note tells me exactly where I was.

Any stop, pause, finish, or done → I leave a note. Always.
