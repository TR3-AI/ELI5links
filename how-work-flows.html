The whole thing in one line

Idea → plan → build → check → ship.
Five steps. You say yes twice: to start it (step 1) and to ship it live (step 4). That live-yes is PR #6, waiting for you now.

⬇️
STEP 1 💡

Your idea → green light

You tell the team's Manager yourself. You two shape it into one clear goal. Then you say yes to begin.

idea→Manager→map it→goal→✋ your YES
⬇️
STEP 2 📄

Turn it into job cards

Two planners write the plan. It grows into full build steps, then small job cards — each one says exactly where it fits. Only the Manager carries each step along.

plan→skeleton→full guide→job cards
⬇️
STEP 3 🔨

Build each card

For every card: write the test first (red), make it pass (green), then tidy up. On to the next card.

RED→GREEN→TIDY↻next
⬇️
STEP 4 ✅

Check & merge

Cards go out in batches of 5. A robot scores each out of 5. A top score lands on the practice stage. Going live needs your yes.

batch of 5→robot score→5/5 → staging→✋ your YES
⬇️
STEP 5 🔁

Day-to-day

Three work blocks a day. Four short reports each block. Ops carries messages to you. Every pause leaves a note, so nothing is ever lost.

work blocks·reports·Ops relays·notes
⬇️
📍

You are here → PR #6

PR #6 is that second yes. It pushes the tested rules from the practice stage to live, so every team gets them. Only you can approve it.

your call: merge #6 to live? 🐶
