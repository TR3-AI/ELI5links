Is our 2% edge rule right? 📏

what one week of real Run 4 bets taught us

1 🏷️

What "edge" means

Our AI guesses what a bet is worth. The market has a price tag. Edge = our guess minus the price tag. Rule today: only buy when the edge is at least 2 cents on the dollar.

⬇️
2 🧾

We checked the receipts

All 304 finished bets from Run 4, matched to how the games really ended. Week's total: bet $10,075, won +$1,049 (about +10%). 65 of every 100 bets won. The system makes money.

⬇️
3 📐

But the ruler is broken

When the AI says "I'm 80–90% sure", it's right only 51 times out of 100. On average it exaggerates by 28 points. So the "edge" number we measure with is mostly hot air.

⬇️
4 🏪

The price tag is the honest ruler

The market's price is almost exactly the true chance. And here's the gold: our picks win ~6 points more often than the price says — on cheap picks and expensive picks alike. That +6 is our real edge.

⬇️
5 🎰

Where wins and losses live

Modest-edge picks are solid favorites: they won 64–87% of the time — steady money. Giant-edge picks are lottery tickets: most lose everything, a few pay 10–20×. Big swings hide there. (Today the stop-loss, not the 2% rule, is what limits damage.)

⬇️
6 ✅

So: is 2% the right number?

Keep it, don't raise it. Every time we tried a higher bar on paper, it deleted profitable bets. And nothing under 2% was ever tried, so nobody knows what's down there. The number isn't the problem — the ruler is.
