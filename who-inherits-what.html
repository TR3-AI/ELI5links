Who inherits what? 🧬

when a team starts — or gets updated — what comes along

1 🧩

Three things, three speeds

"Inherit" hides a split. Three separate things could come along: the rules, the toolkit & code, and a team's private notes. They do not travel together.

⬇️
2 📜

Rules — everyone, on your word

A rule change reaches a new team and the running teams (SM, HAP) alike. That's the refresh: you say "push," and every team re-pulls the rulebook.

⬇️
3 🧰

The toolkit — a new team gets it all

A brand-new team is built from the shared master copy, so it starts with every tool and every bit of code we've finished so far. Full kit, day one.

⬇️
4 ⚠️

…but running teams don't auto-get it

SM and HAP copied the toolkit when they started. New tools we build later don't reach them by themselves — someone has to pull them in. No robot does this in the background.

⬇️
5 🔒

Private notes never travel

Each team keeps its own private notebook. A new team starts empty and fresh; nobody inherits another team's notes. On purpose — they stay separate and safe.

⬇️
6 🧭

The one-line version

Rules fly to everyone. The toolkit rides along only when a team is born or deliberately updated. Private notes never move.
