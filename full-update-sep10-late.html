Your take-anywhere folder

Yes — that folder is ~/.omp. Copy it onto another Mac as ~/.omp and OMP inherits everything: your settings, all profiles, your logins, your plugins.

  • Whole thing = 5.5 GB — you remembered ~5 GB, spot on.
  • Does it include chat history? Yes — about 2.3 GB of it is your saved conversations (3,267 of them).
  • But you don’t need the big version. Your settings + every login + plugins is only ~103 MB. Skip the chat history, the browser files, the logs and an old backup, and you inherit every setting and login in a ~100 MB folder.
  • Cloud: yes — drop that folder in a cloud drive, pull it as ~/.omp on any Mac. Two care points: lock the login file, and note your OMP-5 login token is tied to the machine (the move-guide covers both).
