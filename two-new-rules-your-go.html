Pick-one menus come straight to you

When a tool shows a pick-one-of-these menu, nobody guesses. It jumps straight up to you — agent → manager → Ops → you, right away. You pick; Ops clicks it for you. The team waits for your pick and never wanders off to other work.

⬇️
2 🤫

Quiet mid-block, unless it's a real blocker

Confirmed: between the block reports there are no timed check-ins. A team pings you mid-block only when something truly blocks it — a PR that must merge, a question, an approval, or something only you can do. Otherwise, silence.
