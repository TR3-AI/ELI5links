Decision 5: staging & branches 🌿

one repo · each team its own staging · one central staging → main

1 📦

Each team's branch IS its staging

One repo for the whole project. Each department gets one branch that doubles as its staging — e.g. api-staging — in its own worktree (a separate working copy). No extra staging branch; the department branch and its staging are the same thing.

⬇️
2 🎫

Tickets branch off, PR back in

Each ticket gets its own little worktree off the department's staging (e.g. feat/api/api-004), and its PR merges back into that department's staging at 5/5. Same 5/5 rule you have today — just per department now.

⬇️
3 🪜

The ladder — we keep the word "staging"

ticket → department staging → one central staging → main. No new "project integration" word. Your rules survive word-for-word: today's staging = each department's staging; today's staging→main = the central staging → main.

⬇️
4 🎩

The core team wears two hats

It builds its own section on its own core-staging, exactly like the others. It also owns the central staging where finished departments meet. It never builds its section on the central branch — that stays clean for merging.

⬇️
5 ⚠️

Why not just one shared staging?

If all 3 teams merged into one staging: collisions (one team's break blocks the rest), no clean "done" per department, messy rollback, and muddy waves. A staging per department avoids all four — that's why we split it.

⬇️
6 ✅

One meetup, then main — your OK

The core team merges every finished department into the central staging, runs full-system tests, then PRs to main — your approval, same as today. The end-of-phase PR report still reaches you for any PR stuck below 5/5 after its 7 attempts and splits.

⬇️
7 🗺️

Short names + a map

One repo = one project, so names stay short: staging, api-staging, core-staging, feat/api/api-004. Plus a tiny DEPARTMENTS.md — which branch is which team, what it owns, how it links to core — kept current by the manager.

One toggle left 👇

🏷️

Short names, or project-prefixed?

Signing up for: just how the branches read. Deciding: keep them short (staging, api-staging, feat/api/...) since the repo already is the project — or project-prefix them (typeless-staging) in case a repo ever holds more than one project. Result: naming only, no behaviour change. My pick: short — cleaner, and the repo name already says the project.

Short version: one repo; a staging per team; tickets PR in at 5/5; core builds on its own staging and owns the central one; central staging → main on full-system tests + your OK. Say the word and decision 5 is locked.
