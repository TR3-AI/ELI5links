🤖

A robot grades our work

“Greptile” looks at each tray of finished work and gives it 1 to 5 stars. We want 5 out of 5.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

⬇️
🔁

Try up to 7 times

Not 5 stars yet? We fix it and ask the robot again — up to 7 goes. The second it hits 5/5, we stop.

⬇️
✂️

Still stuck? Chop it up

After 7 goes with no 5/5, we cut the big job into small jobs. Each small job gets its own 7 goes.

⬇️
🙋

Still stuck? Your call

If a small job still can’t reach 5 stars, you pick one:

🔁 keep trying ✅ ship it as-is
⬇️
