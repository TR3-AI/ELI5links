You've already got a few helpers

A tab with some little windows open, each one doing real work. But it's not the full team of nine yet.

⬇️
🔒

Rule #1: your work is safe

It never closes or restarts a window you're using. Nothing inside them gets lost — promise.

⬇️
🏷️

Your windows get a team name tag

Each one you already have gets a team job sticker. Only the name on the outside changes — the work inside stays put.

⬇️
➕

It adds the missing helpers

For every team job you don't have yet, a new window pops in — until the tab has all nine.

⬇️
🤖

Only the new ones start up fresh

The brand-new windows switch on a fresh helper. Your old windows just keep doing their thing.

⬇️
🔢

So, four turns into nine

4 you have ➜ +5 new ➜ 9 total

Your four stay (with new name tags), five fresh ones join. Full team.

⬇️
📐

And it lays out neatly

You end up with a tidy even grid — four on the left, five on the right — same as a fresh full-team tab.

⬇️
🔘

How you run it

On the tab you're in, type /autopane fill.
