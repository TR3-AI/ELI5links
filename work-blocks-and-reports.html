How your teams check in 📅

work blocks, hourly heartbeats, and the “ping the second you’re done” rule

1 📅

The day is 3 work blocks

Everyone works to your day, your time: Block 1  9am–1pm, Block 2  2–5pm, Block 3  6–8pm. Work and reports all line up to these edges.

⬇️
2 🚀

A “spaceship” note before each block

15 minutes before a block starts — 8:45am, 1:45pm, 5:45pm — each team sends a short note: here’s what we did since we last stopped. The 8:45 one is the morning check-in.

⬇️
3 🏁

A wrap-up at each block’s end

At 1pm, 5pm, 8pm each team says what can keep going without you — so nothing waits on you overnight or over a break.

⬇️
4 ⏳

A quick heartbeat every hour

In between blocks, once an hour, each team sends one line: what changed, or “nothing changed”. You never see these — they exist so I catch a stuck team within the hour, not hours later.

⬇️
5 📬

You only get pinged when it’s worth it

I hold reports until 2+ teams have something (a 30-minute window), then send one message. Never one team on its own. And nobody sits idle — if a team waits 30 minutes, it just carries on.

⬇️
6 🔔

Ping the second you’re done

When an agent finishes a job it pings up the chain right away — it never just goes quiet. If a ping I’m owed doesn’t land in ~1 hour, I chase it. And I never trust “done” on someone’s word — I check the real thing myself.
