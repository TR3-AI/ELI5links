Who cuts up the plan? ✂️

the two planners on the core team — once, before anyone builds

1 🙋

Your question

Who cuts the plan into sections, and who breaks those into phases?

⬇️
2 ✌️

The two planners

Planner 1 and Planner 2, both on the core team. The same two do both jobs.

⬇️
3 ⏱️

Once, up front

Right after you approve the plan and the PRD — and before any team starts building.

⬇️
4 ✂️

They cut it two ways

First into sections — one per department, plus one for the core team itself. Then each section into ordered phases.

⬇️
5 🚫

Departments don't cut their own

Those crews are lean — no planners. They get their part already sliced and already phased, handed over in their brief.

⬇️
6 🧩

Two layers

The planners set the boundaries — the sections and the phases. Inside each team, the ticket-writer fills each phase with the actual tasks.

Short version: the core team's two planners slice the plan into sections and break each into phases — once, before anyone builds.
