Every pane wears a name tag

A tab is full of little windows (panes). Each one wears a sticker: the tab's name + its job. Like "Typeless Green Pass" or "Typeless Manager". Front part = tab name. Back part = the job.

⬇️
✏️

Right now, renaming is a chore

If you change the tab's name, the stickers don't change by themselves. You'd have to peel and rewrite every one by hand. Boring.

⬇️
✨

The new trick

We teach it to watch for "the tab got a new name." The second you rename the tab, it runs around and fixes every sticker for you — swaps just the front part, keeps the job.

⬇️
🔤

A quick example

Rename the tab "Typeless" → "Type":

Typeless Green Pass ➜ Type Green Pass

The "Green Pass" job stays the same. Only the front hops from "Typeless" to "Type".

⬇️
🛟

It won't mess anything up

It only fixes the name tags. It doesn't restart your work, move windows, or lose anything. And a window named something that isn't one of our jobs? It's left alone.

so: same windows, same jobs, same work — the name just follows the tab 🐶
